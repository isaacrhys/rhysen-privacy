Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Rhysen iOS app handles information when you use its task, reminder, habit, assistant, work-planning, and location-based features.

Rhysen does not require an account and does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives your tasks, habits, work information, voice recordings, or location history.

Information stored on your device

Rhysen may store information you choose to enter or create, including:

  • Tasks, reminders, subtasks, dates, times, and completion history.
  • Habits, routines, streaks, timers, counters, and reminder preferences.
  • Work schedules, job names, pay settings, commute details, addresses, and geofence coordinates.
  • Appearance, notification, and other app preferences.
  • Assistant requests and local correction data used to improve results within the app.
This information is stored in Rhysen’s app container and is not sent to the Rhysen developer.

Permissions and Apple services

Rhysen requests access only when needed for a feature you choose to use. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Location Used for optional work-arrival and work-departure geofencing, including automatic clock-in and clock-out features you enable.
Microphone Used when you choose voice input. Rhysen does not retain microphone audio recordings.
Speech recognition Used to convert your voice into text through Apple’s speech-recognition services. Processing may occur on-device or through Apple depending on device capabilities and system settings.
Notifications Used to deliver task, reminder, habit, and work-related alerts that you configure.
Maps and geocoding Addresses you enter may be submitted to Apple’s MapKit or geocoding services to find map coordinates and provide address suggestions.

Apple services are governed by Apple’s privacy practices and your device settings.

Collection, tracking, and sharing

Rhysen does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Rhysen does not sell personal information.

Rhysen does not share your app content with the developer or data brokers. Information processed by Apple system services is handled according to Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

Support form submissions

If you contact Rhysen using the support form below, the email address, device information, issue category, and comments you provide are transmitted to and processed by Formspree so the developer can receive and respond to your request.

Support submissions are used only to investigate and respond to the request. They are generally retained for no longer than 90 days after resolution unless longer retention is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete it within Rhysen, reset the applicable feature, or remove the app. Deleting Rhysen removes information from its local app container.

Apple-managed device backups may retain app data according to your iCloud or device-backup settings. Rhysen does not control the retention of those backups.

Security

Rhysen relies on Apple’s app sandbox and iOS security protections to isolate locally stored information. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so keeping your device software and passcode protections up to date is recommended.

Children’s privacy

Rhysen is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-controlled service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Rhysen’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when revisions are published.
